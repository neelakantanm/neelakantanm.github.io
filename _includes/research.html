Analysis & PDE

  1. Comparison theorem for weak solutions of maximally subelliptic PDEs.

    Description

    We establish a comparison principle for viscosity sub/super solutions of a broad class of second-order quasilinear, maximally subelliptic PDEs on general manifolds. Our result strengthens a recent theorem of Manfredi–Mukherjee, which was proved in the setting of Carnot groups. The main purpose is to highlight how maximal subellipticity enables comparison results for weak solutions that parallel the classical elliptic theory.

  2. A regularity theorem for maximally subelliptic PDE, submitted.

    Description

    This paper establishes a sharp regularity theorem for classical solutions to fully nonlinear maximally subelliptic PDEs on non-isotropic Sobolev spaces which extends the regularity theorem for fully nonlinear elliptic PDEs. This complements B. Street's ('22) result for Zygmund–Hölder spaces with an $\epsilon$-loss in regularity. We also obtain regularity theorems for Besov and Triebel-Lizorkin spaces up to a small loss.

  3. Uniform Poincaré inequalities on measured metric spaces.

    With S. Maity. manuscripta math. (2022).

    Description

    We establish uniform Poincaré inequalities on measured metric spaces with controlled geometry ($\delta$-hyperbolicity, lower bound on entropy, doubling metric measure space) with quantitative bounds on the Poincaré constant. We relate the Poincaré constants obtained to growth of discrete subgroups of the isometries of the space, which act on it properly. We show that if $X$ is the universal cover of a compact $CD(K,\infty)$ space with $K\leq 0$, it supports a uniform Poincaré inequality, and the Poincaré constant depends on the growth of the fundamental group of the quotient space.

  4. MS thesis.

    Description

    We prove that the Abel sums of the spectral projections of $L^p(H^n)$ functions on Heisenberg group ($H^n$) converge to the function almost everywhere for $1<p<\infty$ and hence an extension of the $L^p$ spectral theory proposed by R. Strichartz ('91). We prove our result by establishing the $L^p$ boundedness of the Littlewood-Paley $g$-function for the heat semi-group of $(−\mathcal{L}) (iT)^{-1}$, where $\mathcal{L}$ is the Heisenberg sublaplacian and $T=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}$.

  1. A Counterexample to Fourier Alignment in Single-Neuron Modular Addition.

    Description

    We construct a counterexample to single-frequency Fourier alignment for a ReLU neuron trained on modular addition. This gives a negative answer to a question raised in MAIS-60 .
